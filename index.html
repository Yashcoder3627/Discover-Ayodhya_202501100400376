Discover Ayodhya -

Ayodhya is one of India’s most ancient and sacred cities, celebrated as the birthplace of Lord Rama. Situated on the banks of the holy Sarayu River, it blends spiritual devotion with a rich cultural heritage.


Key Attractions-

  • Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir – Iconic temple dedicated to Lord Rama
  • Hanuman Garhi – Hilltop temple with panoramic city views
  • Kanak Bhawan – Beautiful shrine with golden idols
  • Sarayu River Ghats – Peaceful spot for evening aarti

Travel Tips

  • Best time to visit: October to March for pleasant weather
  • Dress modestly and respect local customs at temples.
  • Book hotels early during major festivals such as Ram Navami.
