Ayodhya -

Ayodhya is a historic city in the Indian state of Uttar Pradesh, situated on the banks of the sacred Sarayu River. It is renowned as the birthplace of Lord Rama, the hero of the epic Ramayana, and is one of the seven holiest cities (Sapta Puri) in Hindu tradition.

Historical & Cultural Importance -

  • Ancient Roots: References to Ayodhya appear in texts as old as the Ramayana and Atharva Veda, describing it as the capital of the legendary Kosala kingdom.
  • Religious Significance: The city attracts millions of Hindu pilgrims every year, especially during festivals like Ram Navami and Diwali.

Getting there -

Ayodhya is well connected by road, rail, and air. The new Maharishi Valmiki International Airport provides direct flights to several major Indian cities
